# 🏴‍☠️ One Piece

## About the Series
**One Piece** is a Japanese anime and manga series created by *Eiichiro Oda*.  
It follows the adventures of **Monkey D. Luffy**, a cheerful and determined pirate whose dream is to find the legendary treasure known as the **One Piece** and become the *Pirate King*.

---

## 🌊 Main Characters
- **Monkey D. Luffy** – Captain of the Straw Hat Pirates  
- **Roronoa Zoro** – Swordsman and right-hand man  
- **Nami** – Navigator and weather expert  
- **Usopp** – Sniper and storyteller  
- **Sanji** – Cook with deadly kicks  
- **Tony Tony Chopper** – Doctor and adorable reindeer  
- **Nico Robin** – Archaeologist and historian  
- **Franky** – Shipwright and cyborg  
- **Brook** – Musician and skeleton  
- **Jinbe** – Helmsman and fish-man warrior  

---

##  Favorite Arc
### *Wano Country Arc*
> A thrilling story of samurai, honor, rebellion, and destiny.  
> Luffy and his crew join forces with the rebels of Wano to overthrow Kaido, one of the Four Emperors.
